About DarkZone

Built for simulator players who love realism, routes, and modded experiences.

RTS DarkZone Gaming is a simulator-focused platform for both PC and mobile players. From Euro Truck Simulator 2 to Proton Bus Simulator and Bus Simulator Indonesia, this space is made for discovering gameplay, custom vehicles, map add-ons, skins, and community-driven content.
